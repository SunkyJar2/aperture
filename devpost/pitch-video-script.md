## Part 1: What is This Project? (0:00 – 0:30)

> **Goal:** Hook the judges immediately, define Aperture, and highlight its technical foundation (Gemini Multimodal AI + OpenCV + WebGL).

### 0:00 – 0:10 | The Vision & Hook
* **Visual:** Full-screen shot of the Aperture dark studio interface. A live camera feed is active in the central viewer with live RGB parade scopes undulating underneath.
* **On-Screen Text:** *Aperture — AI Cinematography Studio*
* **Voiceover:**  
  > *"This is Aperture — an AI-powered cinematography studio and virtual co-director that bridges narrative vision with real-time camera telemetry."*

### 0:10 – 0:20 | The Core Architecture
* **Visual:** Camera zooms into the telemetry readouts (Shadows %, Highlights %, Luma) and the film look LUT selector cycling to *Kodak 2383*.
* **Voiceover:**  
  > *"Built with the ergonomics of a professional DaVinci Resolve suite, Aperture pairs real-time OpenCV exposure analytics and hardware WebGL shaders with Google Gemini’s multimodal vision intelligence."*

### 0:20 – 0:30 | Versatile Camera Inputs
* **Visual:** Quick cut showing the pairing QR code on screen and a smartphone streaming wirelessly as an interactive camera source with manual ISO and shutter controls.
* **Voiceover:**  
  > *"Whether using a studio webcam or a wirelessly linked smartphone, Aperture turns any live camera feed into an interactive, AI-assisted directorial stage."*

## Part 2: How This Project Helps Filmmakers (0:30 – 0:55)

> **Goal:** Highlight indie filmmaker challenges (solo shooting, lighting errors, lack of a veteran crew) and show how Aperture solves them on set.

### 0:30 – 0:42 | The Filmmaker's Dilemma
* **Visual:** Montage of common indie mistakes highlighted in Aperture: toggling False Color (`Key: F`) showing blown-out highlights, and Zebra stripes (`Key: Z`) animating over clipped areas.
* **Voiceover:**  
  > *"Independent creators, solo shooters, and film students often work without a Director of Photography or a gaffer. They struggle with lighting ratios, camera angles, and exposure clipping—often discovering ruined takes only in post-production."*

### 0:42 – 0:55 | The On-Set AI Co-Director
* **Visual:** Side-by-side view showing the chat dialogue and the advice panels (*Angles*, *Lighting*, *Composition*).
* **Voiceover:**  
  > *"Aperture solves this right on set. Instead of giving generic tips, it listens to your story intent, analyzes your exact sensor telemetry, and acts as an experienced DP standing right beside you to guide every creative decision."*

## Part 3: Live Demonstration (0:55 – 1:50)

> **Goal:** Walk through the complete core loop: Intent Dialogue → Live Capture → Multimodal Analysis → 2D Lighting Diagram → A/B Comparison.

### 0:55 – 1:07 | Step 1: Setting Scene Intent
* **Visual:** The user types into Director Chat: *"Tense noir interrogation with heavy shadows"*. Hit Enter.
* **Voiceover:**  
  > *"Here’s how it works. First, I tell Aperture my creative intent: a tense noir interrogation. Gemini responds as our Co-Director, asking a sharp clarifying question about character dominance and light motivation."*

### 1:07 – 1:18 | Step 2: Live Frame Capture
* **Visual:** Mouse clicks the amber **ANALYZE FRAME** button (or press `Alt + A`). A brief pulse animation plays while Gemini evaluates the frame and histogram.
* **Voiceover:**  
  > *"Once framed, I click Analyze Frame. Aperture instantly packages the camera snapshot with our OpenCV histogram metrics and sends it to Gemini’s multimodal vision model."*

### 1:18 – 1:35 | Step 3: Structured Advice & 2D Lighting Diagram
* **Visual:** The three Advice Cards populate with crisp, actionable bullet points. The camera pans down to highlight the **2D Top-Down Lighting Setup Diagram**, showing key, fill, and rim light angles and power percentages.
* **Voiceover:**  
  > *"In seconds, we get structured, tailored advice: lowering the camera to eye level, adjusting our key light based on our 12% shadow clipping, and an automated 2D top-down lighting diagram showing exact fixture angles and intensities."*

### 1:35 – 1:50 | Step 4: Verification & Lookbook Export
* **Visual:** The user adjusts a lamp or exposure, logs Take 2 into the bottom Take Reel, and presses `W` to drag the interactive **A/B Split Wipe divider** comparing before and after. Then clicks **EXPORT LOOKBOOK**.
* **Voiceover:**  
  > *"We adjust our practical lights, check our changes against Take 1 with the real-time A/B wipe comparison, and can export a print-ready production lookbook with a single click."*
